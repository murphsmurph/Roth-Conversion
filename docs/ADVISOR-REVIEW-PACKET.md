## 5. For the reviewer's awareness — known modeling divergences
From `tests/unit/legacy-crosscheck.test.ts`. The **validated** engine (optimizer panel) is the
more accurate of the two; the **legacy** engine (year-by-year projection table) differs in two ways:

1. **OBBBA senior deduction** — modeled by the validated engine, **not** by the legacy engine, so
   the legacy table over-states tax for clients 65+.
2. **Dividends in SS provisional income** — included by the validated engine per IRC 86, **omitted**
   by the legacy engine, so the legacy table under-taxes Social Security when SS and dividends
   coincide.

Resolved rule decisions (see `docs/tax-rules/modeling-decisions.md`, `RMD-08-discrepancy.md`):
exact brackets vs. the <$100k Tax Table (≤~$6/yr), full-cents vs. whole-dollar rounding (<$1/line),
and RMD-08 corrected at the generator. No open rule decisions remain.
